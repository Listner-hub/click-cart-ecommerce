How does Cash on Delivery (COD) work? ▼

When you place an order, our representative prepares your shipment. You only pay the rider cash when the parcel arrives at your address.

How long does shipping take? ▼

Major cities (Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad/Rawalpindi) usually receive orders within 2 to 3 working days. Other areas take 3 to 5 business days.

How can I edit or add products as an store owner? ▼

Click the Admin Dashboard button in the top navigation bar. You have full control to edit titles, prices, pictures/emojis, stock levels, and inspect incoming customer orders!
